# Alternative watercolor slides

Generated with the built-in OpenAI ImageGen tool. The supplied image was used only as a style and mood reference; its text, emoji, characters and exact layout were not copied.

## Shared direction

Two original 16:9 landscape alternatives for every CreateKids week, rendered as delicate children's-book watercolor and colored-pencil illustrations on warm ivory paper. Thin sepia contours, translucent peach-beige washes, muted olive, dusty coral and restrained powder-blue accents, visible paper grain and a warm family mood. Variant A uses two caring hands as a cradle or creative space. Variant B uses one hand as a protective arch. No text, letters, numbers, punctuation, emojis, logos or watermarks.

## Weekly subjects

- Week 1 — observation and imagination: geometric shapes become characters; a miniature museum of everyday objects.
- Week 2 — visual invention: an ink blot becomes several creatures; siblings compare different pictures born from the same shapes.
- Week 3 — storytelling: an open path offers multiple endings; siblings arrange wordless story cards.
- Week 4 — rhythm and sound: a household orchestra; a call-and-response rhythm with cups, tubes and spoons.
- Week 5 — construction: testing a cardboard bridge; building a safe five-step chain reaction.
- Week 6 — theater: hands become a small stage; siblings improvise with friendly masks and paper props.
- Week 7 — photo and cinema: a wordless film strip and cardboard camera; one scene filmed in playful and gently suspenseful moods.
- Week 8 — digital creativity: a paper prototype blooms into an interactive garden; siblings invent a cooperative game with abstract shapes.
- Week 9 — creative remix: sound becomes drawing, movement and story; siblings exchange ideas across these media.
- Week 10 — human-centered design: three morning-routine inventions; observing a prototype test and improving it.
- Week 11 — author project: several creative directions converge into one chosen project; independent revision with respectful family support.
- Week 12 — family festival: an intimate exhibition of drawings, prototypes, theater and film; children choose what to share and what to keep private.

## Files

Each week is saved as `week-XX-watercolor-a.jpg` and `week-XX-watercolor-b.jpg`, optimized to 1280×720 JPEG for the web app.
